Timeline

With Sticky timing on, everything after a bar moves with it and bars never overlap. Turn it off, or hold Alt while dragging, to overlap bars or move one without pushing the rest. Drag a bar\u2019s right edge to change how long it takes (double-click resets). Click the ruler to jump.

Selected

Nothing selected. Click an item, or drag across empty space to select several. Shift-click adds or removes one.

Tip: drag the corner squares on the page to resize, and the round handle on top to rotate (snaps to 15 degrees, hold Alt for free).

Drag any selected item to move them all together. The corner squares resize the group and the round handle rotates it. Pick a colour on the left to recolour them all.
